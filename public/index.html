How to play

Aim
Press and drag anywhere on the table and the cue follows. Fine-tune with ◀ ▶ or the arrow keys.
Shoot
Pull the power bar down and let go. You can also drag back from the cue ball and release, or hold the space bar and let go.
Spin
Tap high on the spin ball for follow, low for draw.
Rules
Pot all of your group, solids (1–7) or stripes (9–15), then the 8. The table stays open after the break until someone pots a ball. A scratch, hitting the wrong ball first, or no ball reaching a cushion is a foul, and your opponent gets ball in hand: they drag the cue ball anywhere. Pot the 8 early, or scratch while potting it, and you lose.
