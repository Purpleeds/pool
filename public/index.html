How to play

Controls

Aim
Press and drag anywhere on the table. The cue follows your finger or pointer.
Fine aim
Drag the ridged wheel sideways, or use the arrow keys. Hold Shift for bigger steps.
Shoot
Pull the power slider and let go. You can also drag back from the cue ball and release, or hold the space bar and let go.
Spin
Tap high on the cue ball for follow, low for draw. Double-tap to clear it.
Ball in hand
Drag the cue ball to where you want it, then aim and shoot.

8-ball rules

  • Pot all of your group, solids (1–7) or stripes (9–15), then the 8.
  • The table stays open after the break. The first ball someone pots decides the groups.
  • Pot one of your own balls and you shoot again.
  • A scratch, hitting the wrong ball first, or no ball reaching a cushion is a foul. Your opponent gets ball in hand.
  • Pot the 8 before your group is cleared, or scratch while potting it, and you lose the frame.
